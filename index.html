Why this matters

Registering courses took longer than it should have — two courses booked in minutes, and the third one nowhere in the portal. This tracker keeps every course with its code, teacher, days and times, and credit hours, plus a weekly timetable and a countdown to the registration deadline. A course that is missing from the portal is flagged, so you know exactly who to ask. Your list is saved in your browser.
